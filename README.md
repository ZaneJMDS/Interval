# Interval

2D platformer (made in SFML) where you try to reach the fruit. A final time list will appear in the menu displaying the player's level completion times.



Important:
Modifying the text files will change the level layout.



Needs fixes:

* Holding jump on certain objects will play the sound again.
* Particles still look a little funny / go to the next level.
* Needs vfx for when player enters a fall mode.



Controls:

Movement:

A, <: Move Left

D, >: Move Right

W, Space, ^: Jump

S, <Down arrow>: Fall



Accessability:

R: Reset level

O: Decrease music volume

P: Increase music volume

1-9: level Loader (Only while the debug menu is selected)

ESC: Debug menu toggle



Biomes:

* Forest
* Desert
* Mountain
* Snowy



Tiles:

* Wall - Stops the player
* Box - Can be moved (If moved into a wall, the box will appear on the other side of the player)
* Platforms - Player can jump and fall through them
* Drum - Bounce the player higher
* Spike - Kills the player



Also a slime enemy that moves from side to side and kills the player if they collide.
